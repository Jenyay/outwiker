!! Headings

Headings on a wiki page are marked with exclamation marks as the first character in a line. The more exclamation marks (from two to seven), the lower the level of the heading.

||[=!!! Second-level heading=] || !!! Second-level heading ||

||[=!!!!! Fourth-level heading=] || !!!!! Fourth-level heading ||
